I keep painting empty rooms. Not because there is nothing to paint, but because the emptied places are the ones that can hold looking.

The first study leaves only an ochre wall and a piece of light that has been moved. The second presses the room into a few lines; the small red block is the only furniture that was not erased. The third is a window at night, with no landscape outside, only a green deeper than the room.
